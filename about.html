About St. Mary's School

St. Mary's School, Newasa is an English Medium Convent School managed by the Ursuline Sisters of Mary Immaculate.

Our institution is committed to providing quality education that develops academic excellence together with discipline, leadership and moral values.

We strive to create a safe learning environment where every student is encouraged to discover their talents and become responsible citizens.

Our Journey

History of the School

St. Mary's School, Newasa is an English Medium Convent School managed by the Ursuline Sisters of Mary Immaculate. Inspired by the vision of our foundress, Blessed Brigida Morello, who founded the Congregation of the Ursulines of Mary Immaculate in 1649 in Northern Italy, the school is committed to providing quality education that nurtures faith, culture, knowledge and character. Guided by the Ursuline philosophy of education, we strive for the integral formation of every child, helping students become God-fearing, honest, compassionate and responsible citizens.

St. Mary's School is a Christian minority co-educational institution administered by the Religious Congregation of the Ursuline Sisters of Mary Immaculate and functions under the religious jurisdiction of the Catholic Bishop of Nashik Diocese. Affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education, the school offers a balanced education supported by modern facilities such as computer laboratories and smart classrooms. Along with academic excellence, students are encouraged to participate in sports, yoga, karate, skating, dance, fine arts and various social and charitable activities. Following the noble virtues of Mother Mary and the inspiration of Blessed Brigida Morello, the school continues its mission of developing well-rounded individuals with strong moral values, patriotic spirit and global understanding.

OUR LEADERSHIP

History of Principals

Leadership Through the Years

Since its establishment in 1993, St. Mary's School, Newasa has been guided by visionary and dedicated principals whose commitment to excellence has shaped the institution into a center of quality education, discipline, and moral values.

Each principal has contributed significantly to the school's growth by strengthening academic standards, encouraging holistic development, promoting innovation, and fostering a nurturing environment where students are inspired to learn and succeed.

Today, the school proudly continues this legacy of excellence under the able leadership of Sr. Jyothis Joseph, carrying forward the vision of providing value-based education and preparing students to become responsible global citizens.

7 Principals have successfully led St. Mary's School since its establishment in 1993.
