School Rules & Regulations

These rules help maintain a safe, disciplined and respectful environment where every student can learn and grow. Parents and students are requested to read and follow them carefully.

  General Discipline

  • Students must reach school before the assembly bell and maintain punctuality throughout the day.
  • Students are expected to be regular, and leave applications must be submitted in advance for planned absences.
  • Respect towards teachers, staff, elders and fellow students is expected at all times.
  • Students must maintain silence and discipline during assembly, prayer and class hours.
  • Use of unfair means during examinations or tests will attract strict disciplinary action.
  • Damage to school property will require compensation from the student/parent.

  Attendance & Leave

  • A minimum attendance percentage, as prescribed by the school, is required to appear for examinations.
  • Sick leave of more than three days requires a medical certificate.
  • Prior written permission is required for early dismissal from school.

  Conduct & Prohibited Items

  • Mobile phones and other electronic gadgets are not permitted on school premises unless specifically authorised.
  • Bullying, ragging or any form of misconduct will not be tolerated and may lead to strict action, including suspension.
  • Students must not bring valuables or large sums of money to school.

  Uniform & Appearance

  • Students must wear the complete, correct school uniform on all working days.
  • Neat and tidy appearance, including hairstyle and footwear, is compulsory as per school guidelines.
  • Sports uniform is to be worn only on designated days.
